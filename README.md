# EMF and the Engrafted Word: My Hypothesis, Refined

**By Elijah Echekwu**

*October 7, 2026*

## My Original Assumption

I had always understood electromotive force (EMF) as the force that pushes electrons around a circuit. In my mind, EMF was the thing acting directly on the electron and driving it through the wire.

I was trying to understand this term, and I noticed it resembled something I know from Scripture. The Bible speaks of truth, which is God's word. I reasoned that just as EMF causes the electron to move through the circuit, truth is the motive for the child of God, the new creature. Truth inspires them, moves them and controls them. So I began to read "electromotive force" as a picture of "truth as motive".

## What EMF Really Is

EMF is not a force in the strict sense. It is the energy a source, such as a battery, gives to each unit of charge as it moves that charge from the low-potential terminal to the high-potential terminal inside the source. The name is a leftover from older physics, where "force" meant a driving effort. It is measured in volts, not newtons.

Inside the battery, the electric field pulls charge back down, the way gravity pulls water downhill. A chemical reaction acts like a pump and forces the charge uphill anyway. That pumping effort per unit of charge is the EMF.

The picture that made it clear for me is a hill. A ball at the top has more energy than a ball at the bottom, just from where it sits. The battery carries charge up the hill by hand, and a bigger EMF means a steeper hill. The wire is the path the charge then rolls back down, and that rolling is the current.

## The Corrected Chain

My assumption was backwards. EMF does not push the electron in the wire. It is one step removed, the original cause, and the electric field in the wire is what does the direct pushing. The correct order is:

1. **EMF.** A chemical reaction inside the battery does work on charges, moving them from the negative terminal to the positive terminal against their natural pull. The energy spent per unit of charge is the EMF.
2. **Potential difference.** Charge builds up, positive at one terminal and negative at the other. This separation is the potential difference, the voltage.
3. **Electric field.** The potential difference creates an electric field that spreads through the connecting wire almost instantly.
4. **Force on the electron.** That field exerts a real force on every free electron already in the wire, and the electrons drift together, producing current.

So EMF creates the potential difference, the potential difference creates the field, and the field pushes the electron. The battery does not push one electron that bumps the next. The field is present throughout the wire at once.



The force that moves the electron is F, not ε. EMF sits at the start of the chain as the energy source behind everything that follows.
## The Spiritual Parallel

Once the physics was corrected, my mapping needed refining too. I had treated truth as the direct force that moves the believer. Scripture shows something one layer deeper.

Truth, which is God's word, does not grab a person's hands and feet and force them to act, just as EMF does not grab the electron directly. Instead, the truth is **implanted** in them. James 1:21 speaks of the engrafted word, worked into the person's inner being the way the battery's chemical reaction works on charge inside itself.

That implanted truth reconditions the mind. Through meditation, the truth planted in the heart renews the mind. And the renewed mind is what governs a person's life, because all of a man's life flows from his mind. Proverbs 4:23 says to be careful how you think, since your life is controlled by your mind and your thoughts.

So the truth implanted in a person creates an inner environment, a new orientation, that pushes, guides and controls them. The word is the source. The renewed mind is the field it creates throughout the whole person, not at one point only, the way the electric field fills the whole wire and not just the battery. And the believer's daily conduct is the force that field produces.

## Mapping Side by Side

Each step of the physics chain has a counterpart in the believer's life.

| Step | In the circuit | In the believer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Source | EMF: the battery's chemical work, energy per charge (ε) | The truth, God's word, implanted in the heart (James 1:21) |
| 2. Potential difference | Charge separated, a slope from low to high (V) | A new orientation formed as the truth is meditated on |
| 3. Field | The electric field present all along the wire (E) | The renewed mind, governing the whole person (Proverbs 4:23) |
| 4. Force | The push on each electron, F = qE | Daily thoughts, choices and conduct that the renewed mind drives |

In both columns the source is not what pushes at the end. It works on the inside first, and the push comes through what it creates.

## My Refined Hypothesis

I began by thinking EMF was the force that pushes electrons around the circuit, and I mapped that to truth as the direct motive of the child of God. Studying EMF corrected the physics, and the correction improved the parallel.

EMF is the energy a source gives to each unit of charge. It creates the potential difference, the potential difference creates the electric field, and the field exerts the force that moves the electrons.

In the same way, the truth of God's word does not force a person from outside. It is engrafted into them (James 1:21) and, through meditation, renews the mind. The renewed mind, which governs a person's whole life (Proverbs 4:23), becomes the environment that moves, guides and controls them. Truth is the motive, but it moves the believer through the inner transformation it creates, not by direct force.
